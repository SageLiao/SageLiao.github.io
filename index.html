Published Paper: Master Thesis, Conference Paper, Bachelor Seminar

  • Master Thesis: CAITOD: Commerce AI Multimodal Cross-Language Task-Oriented Dialogue System

    • Due to the rapid development in natural language processing, dialogue systems play an important role in addressing the issues of human-computer interaction, which make them certainly valuable systems for business. AI customer service is a common example of task-oriented dialogue systems in commercial applications. However, most previous studies in task-oriented dialogue systems only focused on text communication and generally accepted one language. Also, relevant research on multimodal cross-language task-oriented dialogue systems is relatively rare. The purpose of this research is to construct a multimodal cross-language task-oriented dialogue system in commercial application, called CAITOD. The research methodology uses transfer learning and a pretrained foundation model to construct a task-oriented dialogue system.
